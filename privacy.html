Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026 · Iyolme ("we", "our", "the app")

This Privacy Policy explains what information Iyolme collects when you use our mobile app and website (iyolme.com, io.iyolme.com), how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

  • Account data: name, username, phone number / email, profile photo, gender, country, date of birth.
  • Content: live streams, reels, stories, posts, comments, messages, and the music/media you upload.
  • Usage data: rooms joined, features used, watch time, interactions (likes, gifts, follows), device model, OS, IP address, approximate location.
  • Transactions: coin purchases, gift history, earnings (Beans), payout account details you submit for withdrawals.
  • Verification: if you apply for a blue tick or withdraw earnings, ID documents you upload for review.
  • Permissions: camera, microphone, storage and contacts — only when you enable features that need them (going live, uploading media, finding friends).

2. How we use your information

  • Run the app: live streaming, rooms, chat, feeds, gifting, wallet and payouts.
  • Personalize your feed, rankings and recommendations.
  • Process payments, subscriptions, withdrawals and creator earnings.
  • Safety & moderation: fraud prevention, content compliance, account security.
  • Service messages: transaction receipts, security alerts, feature updates.

3. Sharing

  • Your public profile, live streams, reels, stories and posts are visible to other users.
  • We share data with service providers that run the app for us: cloud storage/CDN, streaming infrastructure (Agora, LiveKit), push notifications, and payment processors.
  • We may disclose data if required by law or to protect users' safety and our rights.
  • We do not sell your personal data.

4. Retention

We keep your data while your account is active. Stories expire automatically after 24 hours. Payout and transaction records are retained as required for accounting and legal compliance.

5. Your rights

  • Edit your profile and privacy settings anytime in the app.
  • Request a copy of your data or account deletion via Delete Account or by emailing support@iyolme.com.
  • Revoke device permissions in your phone settings (some features will stop working).

6. Children

Iyolme is for users 18+ (or the age of majority in your region). We do not knowingly collect data from minors; accounts found to belong to minors are removed.

7. Security

We use encryption in transit, access controls and session protections. No system is 100% secure — keep your password and OTP private.

8. Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version. Continued use of the app means you accept the updated policy.

9. Contact

Questions or requests: support@iyolme.com
