Delete your Iyolme account

You can delete your account in two ways:

Option 1 — In the app (fastest)
  1. Open the Iyolme app → Profile → Settings
  2. Tap Account → Delete Account
  3. Confirm — your account is deactivated immediately and erased after a 30-day grace period.
Option 2 — Email request

Email support@iyolme.com from your registered email/phone with subject "Delete my account" and include your Iyolme user ID / username. We process requests within 7 days.

Warning: deletion removes your profile, followers, content, Diamonds and Beans balances permanently. Pending payouts must be settled before deletion.
