FAQ

Questions? Answers.

The stuff everyone asks before hitting download.
Is Iyolme free to use?
Yes — downloading, watching lives, posting reels and chatting are completely free. Optional in-app purchases (Diamonds, VIP) unlock gifts, boosts and premium privileges.
How do creators earn money?
Creators earn Beans from gifts received on lives, PK battles, audio rooms and boosted content. Beans convert to real money and can be withdrawn via bank transfer, UPI or PayPal once you hit the minimum threshold.
What are PK Battles?
PK Battles pit two live hosts against each other for a fixed time. Fans send gifts to push their host's score — the higher score wins. It's the fastest way to grow and earn on Iyolme.
Is the app safe?
Yes. Iyolme has report & block tools, moderator controls in rooms, AI-assisted host compliance, username restrictions and verified badges. You control who can view, follow or message you.
How do I get the blue verified badge?
Apply for verification inside the app. Once approved by our team, the blue tick appears on your profile, lives and comments.
